# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming experience
for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Our Standards

**Do:**

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community

**Don't:**

- Use sexualized language or imagery
- Troll, insult, or make derogatory comments
- Harass anyone, publicly or privately
- Publish others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or
contacting the maintainers directly.
